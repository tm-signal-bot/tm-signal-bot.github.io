Operation and recovery

Usage notes

These notes explain how to use this private utility. They are operational information, not a separate legal contract or public service offer.

Intended use

TM Signals VPS Backups is for the operator to back up data they control to a Google account they authorize. It is not open for public sign-up. Google access should remain limited to drive.file for app-created or explicitly selected and authorized files.

There are no actions to connect an account or upload data on these information pages.

Before enabling scheduled backups

The operator must verify the destination, complete authorization, protect the recovery key separately and test retrieval of an encrypted backup. A prepared or installed backup job is not proof that a usable off-server backup exists.

Keep access tokens private. Do not include the Google OAuth token or the repository password in the backup archive. Review permissions and access when the operator or server changes.

What a backup does

A backup preserves the data included at the time it was made. It cannot recover changes made after that point, and it does not automatically move the application to another server.

Availability depends on the server, network, Google authorization and storage capacity. Successful checks help establish that a particular backup is usable; they do not guarantee that every future backup or recovery will succeed.

Before restoring, keep the recovered application stopped and review any activity newer than the chosen snapshot so it is not repeated unintentionally.

Stopping or removing access

The operator can disable the backup schedule and revoke the app’s Google access. Existing backups remain until they are manually deleted; automatic remote pruning is not enabled. Keep a usable recovery copy before deleting the last backup.

Questions about operation or access should go to the support contact shown on the Google consent screen. The privacy page describes storage and deletion in more detail.
